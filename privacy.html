Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 10, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy ("Policy") governs how Loop ("Loop", "we", "us") collects, uses, stores, and discloses information obtained through your use of our Discord bot and related services (the "Service"). By adding Loop to a server or otherwise using the Service, you acknowledge that you have read and agree to this Policy. If you believe any part of this Policy is inaccurate, please contact us at privacy@loopbot.app.

2. What Loop Collects

To operate the Service, we collect and process the following categories of data:

2.1. User Identifiers — Discord user IDs and usernames.

2.2. Server (Guild) Information — server IDs and server names.

2.3. Channel and Role Data — channel IDs and role IDs, used to apply settings and permissions correctly.

2.4. Message Data — message content and message IDs, processed for moderation purposes such as detecting spam, filtered terms, or rule violations.

2.5. Command Data — the commands you invoke and the arguments supplied to them, used to execute the requested action.

3. Why We Collect It

We collect and process this data solely for legitimate operational purposes, including:

3.1. Facilitating command execution and core bot functionality;

3.2. Enforcing moderation rules and protecting servers from spam, raids, or abuse;

3.3. Diagnosing technical issues and maintaining the reliability of the Service;

3.4. Supporting specific opt-in features that server administrators enable.

4. How Long We Keep It

We retain data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in Section 3, or as required by law. Message content processed for moderation is not retained longer than necessary to complete that check. Removing Loop from a server halts further collection for that server, though some data may briefly persist in backups before deletion.

5. Who Else Sees It

5.1. We do not sell, trade, rent, or otherwise transfer User information to external parties.

5.2. We may share information with infrastructure providers (such as hosting or database providers) strictly to operate the Service, or disclose it where required by law or legal process.

6. Managing Your Data

6.1. Deleting Your Data — You may request deletion of your data by emailing privacy@loopbot.app with sufficient information to verify account ownership. Requests are processed within fourteen (14) days.

6.2. Requesting a Copy — You may request a copy of the data we hold about you by emailing the same address. We aim to respond within seven (7) days.

6.3. Turning Things Off — Server administrators may disable specific features or remove Loop from their server at any time to stop further data collection.

7. Age Requirements

The Service is intended for use in accordance with Discord's Terms of Service, which sets minimum age requirements for users. We do not knowingly collect data from users who do not meet those requirements.

8. Changes To This Page

8.1. We may modify, amend, or update this Policy at any time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the Effective Date above.

8.2. Continued use of the Service after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised Policy.
